## Scope and selection

This library collects foundational arguments, major theoretical approaches, empirical findings, quantitative methods, reviews, and critiques. It is not tailored to a particular application or project. Animal consciousness, sleep, anesthesia, memory, and bodily self-awareness are included as cross-cutting topics.

This is a broad reading collection, **not an exhaustive or systematic review**. Availability of public full text affects the selection, with substantial representation from open-access journals. Inclusion is not an endorsement, a ranking of quality, or evidence of consensus. The original papers should be read for assumptions, methods, limitations, corrections, and competing interpretations.

The notes are short topic descriptions, not full critical appraisals. Reviews, philosophical arguments, computational proposals, and empirical studies are labeled separately. Repository copies may be revised after their original submission year. Public access does not waive copyright or license terms.

## Collection checks and maintenance

Every included file was checked with a PDF parser and has readable page structure. Titles and authors were checked against publisher/author pages or first-page text; this does not constitute a full-text scientific review or visual inspection of every page. Failed web responses and empty downloads are excluded from the collection.

Add new papers under the closest field, with a descriptive year-prefixed filename. Record citation, article type, exact source, and a neutral topic note. Keep unavailable items separate and avoid duplicate copies of the same principal work. A file can be relevant to more than one field without being duplicated.
